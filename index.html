Key Sector Risks

Current threats to the banking system
  • 01
    Moody's Negative Outlook — 7 Thai Banks

    Moody's revised outlook to NEGATIVE for 7 major Thai banks in April 2025, tied to sovereign fiscal concerns and household debt levels.

    Negative
  • 02
    NPL Cliff Risk — Potential Rise to 3.73%+ in 2026

    FSSIA estimates system NPL could reach 3.73%+ if Stage 2 (Special Mention) loans deteriorate. Elevated restructured portfolios remain vulnerable.

    Elevated
  • 03
    Rate Cut Cycle Compresses NIM

    BOT cut policy rate to 1.50%; further cuts expected. Rate compression narrows net interest margins and reduces deposit yields across the sector.

    Watch
  • 04
    Household Debt-to-GDP ~91% — Structural Drag

    Elevated household leverage remains a structural risk to consumer credit quality. Limits consumption recovery and increases default sensitivity to rate changes.

    Structural
  • 05
    Fitch Downgrades 2025 Sector Outlook to "Deteriorating"

    Fitch revised its 2025 Thai banking sector outlook from "Neutral" to "Deteriorating", citing asset quality pressures, thin margins, and weak credit demand.

    Negative
